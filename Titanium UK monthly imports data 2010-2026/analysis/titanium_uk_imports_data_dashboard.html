Readiness

Is the dataset ready for Model A?

Weight anomalies: unresolved

Review flagged partner records, especially Italy / HS 810890 / July 2011, before deriving price targets. Train sensitivity variants with the record retained, corrected only from authoritative evidence, or excluded with an audit flag.

GBP conversion: still required

UN Comtrade trade value is USD. Join a time-correct monthly USD-to-GBP exchange-rate series before calculating the final GBP/kg target.

External predictors: still required

Add World Bank monthly nickel and aluminium prices, USGS annual titanium sponge price with its publication lag, and explicit source-availability dates. These are required by the Model A specification.
